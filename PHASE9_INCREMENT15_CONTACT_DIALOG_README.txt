CampusMart Phase 9 Messaging Increment 15 — WhatsApp Contact Dialog

What this update changes:
- After a buyer reveals the seller's WhatsApp number, a dialog appears.
- The dialog asks whether the buyer wants to contact on WhatsApp, make a direct call, or cancel.
- The WhatsApp feature still depends on the reveal_whatsapp_from_conversation Supabase function from Increment 14.

No new Supabase SQL is required for this update.
